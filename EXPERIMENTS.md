# HealthSense Experiments

All results below were generated by actual offline training runs on 2026-09-07.
No result is simulated. Test metrics are from held-out data and model selection
used stratified 5-fold cross-validation on the training portion.

## Shared protocol

- Random seed: 42
- Holdout: stratified 80/20 train/test split
- Candidate models: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting
- Preprocessing: fitted inside sklearn pipelines on training folds only
- Selection: highest cross-validation ROC-AUC, then F1 as tie-breaker
- Metrics: accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, confusion matrix
- Positive class: Diabetes `Outcome=1`; Heart Disease `num>0`; CKD `class=ckd`.
- Confusion matrices use `[[true negative, false positive], [false negative, true positive]]`.

## Calibration and sensitivity

Raw `predict_proba()` outputs were assessed but not calibrated:

- Diabetes: Brier 0.1755 +/- 0.0186; expected calibration error 0.1054 +/- 0.0244
- Heart Disease: Brier 0.0956 +/- 0.0130; expected calibration error 0.1020 +/- 0.0259
- CKD: Brier 0.0094 +/- 0.0040; expected calibration error 0.0432 +/- 0.0071

The Diabetes zero-as-missing policy versus raw-zero retention produced mean
ROC-AUC 0.8135 versus 0.8129 and mean F1 0.6030 versus 0.6198 across the five
holdout seeds. The difference does not establish which representation is correct.

CKD retained no exact feature duplicates and had zero exact train/test feature
overlap under seed 42. Its perfect seed-42 score fell to 0.9900 mean accuracy
across five seeds, while CV ROC-AUC remained 0.9996. Strong univariate and
categorical separation means the result remains a scientific warning.
